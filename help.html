💡 How It Works

Everything you need to know about using PrintDesk.

1

Create an Account

Click Register on the login page and fill in your name, email, and password. Your password must be at least 6 characters. Once registered, you can log in immediately.

💡 Use a real email format — e.g. yourname@example.com
2

Submit a Print Request

From your dashboard, click New Print Job. Fill in:

  • Document Name — The name of the file you want printed (e.g. Assignment_2.pdf)
  • Number of Copies — How many printed copies you need (minimum 1)
  • Print Type — Choose Black & White (cheaper) or Color
  • Page Range — Enter 1-5 for pages 1 to 5, or 2,4,6 for specific pages. Leave blank to print all pages.

Click Submit Request and your job is queued instantly.

💡 You can submit multiple requests — each gets a unique Request ID.
3

Track Your Order

Go to My Orders from the navbar or dashboard. You'll see a table of all your requests with their current status:

Pending — Your request has been received and is waiting for the admin to process it.
Processing — Your document is currently being printed.
Completed — Your printout is ready! Go collect it from the print desk.
💡 Refresh the page to see the latest status updates from the admin.
4

Collect Your Printout

Once your order status shows Completed, visit the print desk in person and quote your Request ID (visible in the My Orders table) to collect your documents.

💡 Screenshot your Request ID just in case!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel a print request? ▼
Currently, requests cannot be cancelled once submitted. If you made a mistake, contact the admin directly and quote your Request ID.
How long does printing take? ▼
Processing time depends on the queue. You'll see the status change from Pending → Processing → Completed on your Orders page. Refresh to check the latest update.
What file types are supported? ▼
Currently you enter the file name manually (e.g. report.pdf). Accepted formats at the print desk are typically PDF, DOCX, and PPTX. Check with your admin for specifics.
I forgot my password. What do I do? ▼
Password reset is not yet available in this version. Please contact the admin to reset your account, or register with a new email.
Is my data saved if I close the browser? ▼
Yes — all requests and user accounts are stored in your browser's localStorage, so they persist across sessions on the same device. Clearing browser data will erase them.
